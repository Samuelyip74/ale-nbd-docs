About Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise (ALE)
=====================================

Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise (ALE) is a global provider of networking, communications,
and cloud solutions serving enterprises across education, healthcare, government,
transportation, and hospitality industries. With a heritage of innovation and
engineering excellence, ALE designs technologies that are trusted in mission-critical
and large-scale environments around the world.

**Global Presence and Heritage**
   - Headquartered in France with operations in over 50 countries.
   - Serving more than **1,000,000+ customers worldwide**, including government agencies,
     airports, universities, hospitals, and financial institutions.
   - Solutions delivered through a global network of over 3,400 partners and resellers.

**Networking Portfolio**
   - **OmniSwitch LAN Portfolio**: High-performance switching platforms delivering
     secure and resilient wired infrastructure for campus, data center, and industrial networks.
   - **OmniAccess Stellar WLAN**: Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 access solutions offering high-density
     performance, AI-based optimization, and integrated security services.
   - **OmniVista Network Management**: Unified management platform (on-prem or SaaS)
     for configuration, monitoring, automation, and network analytics.

**Innovation Focus**
   - Zero Trust security architecture with identity-based access and micro-segmentation.
   - AI-driven operations (AIOps) and automated provisioning for simplified network lifecycle.
   - Support for mission-critical environments, including rail transport, healthcare,
     public safety, and industrial automation.

**Commitment to Sustainability**
   - ALE is committed to reducing environmental impact through energy-efficient
     hardware designs and circular lifecycle programs.
   - Many OmniSwitch models are certified with extended temperature support and
     eco-sustainability compliance.

By choosing ALE technologies, this campus network architecture benefits from a
mature, secure, and future-ready networking platform supported by enterprise-grade
hardware, software, and global service capabilities.
