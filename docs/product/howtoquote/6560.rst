OmniSwitch 6560 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6560 series is designed for enterprise campus and branch environments, offering modular power,
stacking, MACSec, Multi-Gigabit access, and PoE++ support for next-generation Wi-Fi and IP devices.

**Gigabit Models**

OS6560-24X4
   24 × 10/100/1000 RJ-45 access ports, 2 × 1G SFP, 4 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Fixed internal AC power supply. No PoE support.

OS6560-P24X4
   24 × 10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+ ports, 2 × 1G SFP, 4 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Modular AC PSU (600 W). Provides up to 600 W PoE power.

OS6560-48X4
   48 × 10/100/1000 RJ-45 access ports, 2 × 1G SFP, 4 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Fixed internal AC power supply. No PoE support.

OS6560-P48X4
   48 × 10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+ ports, 2 × 1G SFP, 4 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Modular AC PSU (920 W). Provides up to 920 W PoE power.

OS6560-X10
   High-density uplink/aggregation model with 8 × 10G SFP+ and 2 × 20G QSFP+ stacking ports.  
   Fixed internal AC power supply. No copper access ports.

**Multi-Gigabit Models (Wi-Fi 6/6E Ready)**

OS6560E-P24Z8
   24 × 10/100/1000 PoE+ ports, 4 × 2.5G and 4 × 5G Multi-Gig PoE++ (up to 95 W), 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink ports.  
   Internal 600 W AC power supply. Ideal for Wi-Fi 6/6E access points.

OS6560-P24Z24 / OS6560-PXZ24
   24 × 2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE++ ports (802.3bt), 4 × 1G/10G SFP+, and 2 × 20G stacking ports.  
   Bundled with either 600 W (P24Z24) or 920 W (PXZ24) AC PSU.

OS6560E-P48Z16
   48 × Multi-Gig PoE++ ports (12 × 2.5G, 4 × 5G), 4 × 1G/10G SFP+, 2 × 20G stacking ports.  
   Includes 920 W AC PSU for high-density PoE deployments.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What is the difference between OS6560 and OS6560E models?**  
A: OS6560E models include Multi-Gig (2.5G/5G) Ethernet ports and support higher PoE (802.3bt / 95 W) for next-generation 
Wi-Fi access points. 

**Q: Does the OmniSwitch 6560 support MACSec?**  
A: Yes. OS6560-48X4, OS6560-P48X4, and OS6560E-P48Z16 support IEEE 802.1AE MACSec on all access and uplink ports.

**Q: How do I enable full OSPF routing?**  
A: Install the **OS6560-SW-AR** Advanced Routing license to enable extended OSPFv2/v3 scalability and routing features.

**Q: Can the OmniSwitch 6560 be managed via OmniVista Cirrus cloud?**  
A: Yes. Use **OVCX-65-BAS** (Base) or **OVCX-65-BIZ** (Business) licenses for secure cloud-based management and analytics.
