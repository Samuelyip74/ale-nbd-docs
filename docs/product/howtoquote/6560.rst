OmniSwitch 6560 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6560 series is designed for enterprise campus and branch deployments, offering modular power, 
stacking, MACSec, Multi-Gig, and PoE++ capabilities.

**Gigabit Models**

- **OS6560-24X4**  
  24x 10/100/1000 RJ-45, 2x 1G SFP, 4x 1G/10G SFP+, fixed internal AC PSU. No PoE.

- **OS6560-P24X4**  
  24x 10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+, 2x 1G SFP, 4x 1G/10G SFP+, modular AC PSU (600W). PoE up to 600W.

- **OS6560-48X4**  
  48x 10/100/1000 RJ-45, 2x 1G SFP, 4x 1G/10G SFP+, fixed internal AC PSU. No PoE.

- **OS6560-P48X4**  
  48x 10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+, 2x 1G SFP, 4x 1G/10G SFP+, modular AC PSU (920W). PoE up to 920W.

- **OS6560-X10**  
  8x 10G SFP+ ports, 2x 20G QSFP+ stacking. High-density uplink or aggregation switch.

**Multi-Gigabit Models (Wi-Fi 6/6E Ready)**

- **OS6560E-P24Z8**  
  24x 1G PoE, 4x 2.5G, 4x 5G Multi-Gig PoE++ (95W), 2x 10G SFP+. 600W AC PSU.

- **OS6560-P24Z24 / OS6560-PXZ24**  
  24x 2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE++, 4x 10G SFP+, 2x 20G stacking. 600W or 920W PSU bundle.

- **OS6560E-P48Z16**  
  48x Multi-Gig PoE++ (2.5G / 5G mix), 4x 10G SFP+, 2x 20G stacking. 920W PSU.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What is the difference between OS6560 and OS6560E models?**  
A: OS6560E models support Multi-Gigabit (5G), up to 4 ports.

**Q: Does OS6560 support MACSec?**  
A: OS6560-48X4, OS6560-P48X4 and OS6560E-P48Z16 supports MACSec on all RJ-45 ports and uplink ports.

**Q: How do I enable full OSPF routing?**  
A: Install the **OS6560-SW-AR** Advanced Routing license for extended OSPF capabilities.

**Q: Can OmniSwitch 6560 be managed via OmniVista Cirrus cloud?**  
A: Yes. Use **OVCX-65-BAS** (Base) or **OVCX-65-BIZ** (Business) licenses.
