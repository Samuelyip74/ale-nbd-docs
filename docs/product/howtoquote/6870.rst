OmniSwitch 6870 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6870 is a premium enterprise switch family designed for high-density Multi-Gigabit access, 
Wi-Fi 6/6E deployment, and fabric-enabled core/aggregation roles. It supports advanced hardware features such as 
25/100G uplinks, MACsec, SPB/VXLAN fabric, MPLS, and high PoE++ delivery for next-generation edge infrastructure.

**Family Model Categories**

- **Z Series — Multi-Gig Access Edge (Wi-Fi 6/6E)**  
  High-density PoE++, optimized for access layer with 2.5G/5G ports.

- **M Series — Premium Multi-Gig with Higher Uplink Capacity**  
  Expanded uplinks (40/100G), AI telemetry, advanced fabric services.

- **V Series — Fabric Core/Aggregation Variants**  
  High uplink capacity, MPLS/SPB-ready, positioned for core/aggregation.

**Access / Multi-Gig Models (Z Series)**

OS6870-P24Z
   24 × 1G/2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE+ / PoE++ (802.3bt up to 90W),  
   2 × SFP+ (10G) and 2 × QSFP uplinks (25G/40G depending on model).  
   Designed for Wi-Fi 6/6E APs and powered edge devices.

OS6870-P48Z
   48 × 1G/2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE+/PoE++ access ports,  
   2 × 10G SFP+ uplinks, 2 × QSFP (25G/40G) uplinks or stacking.  
   Ideal for high-density campus wireless and enterprise edge.

**Premium Multi-Gig / Fabric Models (M and V Series)**

OS6870-P48M
   48 × 1G/2.5G/5G/10G Multi-Gig PoE++ ports with advanced QoS,  
   2 × 10G SFP+ uplinks, 2 × 40G/100G QSFP uplinks.  
   AI-telemetry enabled, supports SPB/MPLS and Fabric Connect.

OS6870-V12
   Aggregation/Core variant with 12 × 25G SFP28 or modular uplink slots (100G ready),  
   No PoE. Designed for collapsed core, SPB/MPLS fabric, and backbone services.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: Does OmniSwitch 6870 support MACsec encryption?**  

A: Yes. MACsec (802.1AE) is supported on copper and fiber uplink ports with an optional MACsec license, OS-SW-MACSEC.
Note: MACsec not supported on OS6870-24 VFL stacking port 25/26 & OS6870-48 VFL stacking port 49/50.

**Q: Does OmniSwitch 6870 supports Fast/ Perpetual PoE ?

A: Yes, OmniSwitch 6870 supports both Fast PoE and Perpetual PoE features to optimize power delivery and management for connected devices.

**Q: Can 6870 be used for Wi-Fi 6/6E high-power access points?**  

A: Yes. Premium models of OmniSwitch 6870 support PoE (802.3bt) delivering up to 95W, ideal for next-generation APs, cameras, and IoT.
Advanced models support PoE (802.3bt) delivering up to 60W per port.

**Q: How is OmniVista support handled?**  

A: OS6870 can be managed via **OmniVista Cirrus 10 (OVCX licenses)** for cloud-based provisioning, monitoring, and AI analytics.  
On-prem OmniVista 2500 is also supported.

**Q: Does OS6870 support DC power variants?**  

A: Yes.

**Q: What stacking or virtual chassis capabilities exist?**  

A: The 6870 supports Virtual Chassis (VC) stacking up to 8 units via VFL ports, enabling simplified management and redundancy.
