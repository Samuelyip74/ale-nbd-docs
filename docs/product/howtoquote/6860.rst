OmniSwitch 6860 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6860 series is a high-performance, enterprise-grade LAN switch family designed for 
high-density access, Wi-Fi 6/6E edge, and secure campus aggregation. These switches support 
Multi-Gigabit access, advanced PoE++, MACsec, SPB-M, MPLS, and full fabric services with 
hardware-accelerated performance.

**High-Density Multi-Gigabit (Wi-Fi 6/6E Edge)**

OS6860-P24Z
   24 × 2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE+ (802.3at) and PoE++ (802.3bt) ports,  
   2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplinks, 2 × 20G QSFP+ stacking/uplink ports.  
   Supports up to 90W per port (PoE++), ideal for Wi-Fi 6/6E APs.

OS6860-P48Z
   48 × 2.5G/5G Multi-Gig PoE+(802.3at)/PoE++ (802.3bt) access ports,  
   2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplinks, 2 × 20G QSFP+ stacking/uplinks.  
   Designed for dense wireless edge and high-power IP devices.

OS6860-P48M
   48 × 1G/2.5G/5G/10G Multi-Gig PoE++ ports (full-speed),  
   2 × 1G/10G SFP+, 2 × 40G/100G QSFP uplinks (depending on model variant).  
   Highest-end 6860 access switch with expanded uplink capacity and QoS hardware acceleration.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What licenses are required for advanced routing and fabric services?**  

A: No extra license is required for advanced routing such as OSPF/ISIS/SPB. However, OS6860N-MPLS-1 is required for MPLS.

**Q: Does the OmniSwitch 6860 support MACsec encryption?**  

A: Yes. All OS6860 Multi-Gig models support IEEE 802.1AE MACsec on copper and fiber uplink ports with 
the appropriate license. OS-SW-MACSEC is required to enable MACsec functionality.

**Q: Can OS6860 be used for Wi-Fi 6/6E high-power access points?**  
A: Yes. Advanced and Premium model support PoE++ (802.3bt up to 90W), whereas enhanced model support PoE++ (802.3bt up to 60W) ideal for Wi-Fi 6/6E APs, PTZ cameras, and LED PoE lighting systems.
lighting systems.

**Q: What stacking capacity do OS6860 switches provide?**  

A: OS6860 supports Virtual Chassis with up to 8 units using QSFP (20G/40G/100G) for stacking. 

**Q: Can OS6860 be managed in the cloud?**  

A: Yes. Use **OVCX-65-BAS** or **OVCX-65-BIZ** license to manage 6860 via OmniVista Cirrus 10. On-prem 
OmniVista 2500 is also supported.

**Q: Does OS6860 support SPB-M and Fabric Connect?** 

A: Yes. SPB-M (Shortest Path Bridging - MAC) is supported without any additional license, enabling 
zero-touch Layer 2/3 fabric services.

**Q: Are there DC variants available for OS6860?**  

A: Yes. OS6860-BP-D modular 150W DC power supply provides power to one non-PoE OS6860, OS6860E or OS6860N-U28 switch.
