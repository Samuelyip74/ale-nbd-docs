OmniSwitch 6865 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6865 series is a fully hardened, industrial-grade Ethernet LAN switch family designed for mission-critical environments such as transportation, power utilities, roadside cabinets, and heavy industry. These models support extended temperature operation, dual AC/DC power options, precision timing (IEEE 1588v2 PTP), SPB-M, robust PoE, and comprehensive security capabilities.

**Hardened & Industrial Features (Common Across All Models)**

- Operating Temperature: **-40°C to +74°C**
- SPB-M (Shortest Path Bridging - MAC) and OSPF support with license
- IEEE 1588v2 Precision Time Protocol (PTP) support
- DIN rail or 19" rack mounting
- AC and/or DC power supply variants (depending on model)
- Industrial EMC compliance (EN50121-X)

**Available Models**

OS6865-P16X (AC / DC)
   Hardened PoE model with:
   - 12 × RJ-45 Gigabit PoE+/PoE++ access ports (4 high-power PoE++ up to 75W)
   - 2 × 1G SFP and 2 × 10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports
   Supports AC or DC redundant power input, SPB-M, MACsec (license), and PTP timing.

OS6865-U12X (AC / DC)
   Fiber-focused industrial aggregation switch with:
   - 8 × 1G SFP and 2 × 10G SFP+ uplink ports
   - 2 × dual-speed 1G/10G SFP+ ports for stacking or backbone
   Ideal for fiber rings and SPB core in industrial networks. Supports AC or DC PSU.

OS6865-U28X (AC / DC)
   High-port fiber aggregation model with:
   - 24 × 1G SFP access ports
   - 4 × 10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports
   Designed for aggregation and ring architectures with SPB-M, PTP, and dual-power capability.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: Is the OmniSwitch 6865 suitable for outdoor or roadside deployment?**  

A: Yes. The OS6865 is fully hardened (-40°C to +74°C), supports DC power input, and complies with EN50121 railway EMC standards. It is suitable for cabinets, tunnels, substations, and transport waysides.

**Q: Does OS6865 support MACSec encryption?**  

A: MACSec is not supported on OmniSwitch 6865.

**Q: What routing features are available, and do I need a license?**  

A: OmniSwitch 6868 supports dynamic routing protocol such as OSPF/ISIS, no extra license is required.

**Q: Does OS6865 support Precision Time Protocol (PTP)?**  

A: Yes. IEEE 1588v2 end-to-end mode is supported. IEEE 1588v2 peer-to-peer mode is not supported. 

**Q: Can OS6865 be managed via OmniVista Cirrus Cloud?**  

A: Yes. Use **OVCX-65-BAS** or **OVCX-65-BIZ** licenses for cloud-based provisioning, analytics, and remote monitoring.

**Q: What power configurations are available (AC/DC)?**  

A: All OS6865 models support AC/DC power supplies, Dual inputs is recommended for redundancy.
