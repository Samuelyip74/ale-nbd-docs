OmniSwitch 6465 Models
------------------

The OmniSwitch 6465 series includes several models to suit different deployment scenarios. 
Below are the key models and their features:

- OS6465-P6: Hardened GigE fan-less switch. 4x10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+ (2x60W PoE), 2x100/1000 Base-X SFP,RS-232 Console, Alarm relay: 1 In 1 out & USB port. Includes user manual, access card & TS-35/7.5 /15 DIN rail mounting hardware. Order Power Supply separately.  
- OS6465H-P12: Hardened GigE fan-less switch. 8x10/100/1000 BaseT RJ-45 802.3bt type-3 PoE, 4x100/1000 BaseX SFP, alarm relay, RS-232 console & USB ports. FPoE/PPoE and upto 240W PoE budget. Includes user manual & DIN mounting hardware. Order power supply separately.  
- OS6465-P28: Hardened GigE fan-less 1RU switch. 22x10/100/1000Base-T PoE+ ports (8x60W), 2x100/1000 Base-X SFP, 4 x 1G/10G SFP+, RS-232 Console, 1 USB and Alarm relay: 1 In, 1 out.Includes user manuals and 19" rack mount kit. PSU must be ordered separately 
- OS6465-P28D: Hardened GigE fan-less 1RU switch. 22x10/100/1000Base-T PoE+ ports(8x60W), 2x100/1000 Base-X SFP, 4x 1G/10G SFP+, RS-232 Console, 1 USB and Alarm relay: 1 In, 1 out. Includes 24/48V DC PSU, PS tray, user manuals and 19" rack mount kit  
- OS6465T-12: OS6465T-12 GigE chassis. 8 RJ45 10/100/1000 BaseT, 2 Gig combo, 2 SFP ports. 1RU x 1/2 rack, internal AC PSU. Includes a country specific power cord, manuals/SW access card, RJ45-DB9 adaptor. Mounting kits and rubber tabletop feet to be ordered separately  
- OS6465T-P12: OS6465T-P12 GigE chassis. 8 RJ45 10/100/1000 BaseT PoE+, 2 Gig combo, 2 SFP ports. 1RU x 1/2 rack, internal AC PSU. Includes a country specific power cord, manuals/SW access card, RJ45-DB9 adaptor. Mounting kits and rubber tabletop feet ordered separately

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------
**Q: What is the difference between T and non-T model?**  

A: The Alcatel-Lucent OmniSwitch 6465T-12 and 6465T-P12 models are power and acoustically optimized. 
The T model operates without a fan up to 45°C ambient temperature and with a fan can operate up to 60°C.
The 6465T models are designed for standard IT environments and include an internal power supply without power 
redundancy. Wherease the OS6465 switches are a family of hardened, compact, fan-less gigabit Ethernet switches 
that have been designed specifically for industrial applications.  The 6465 models are fanless model that can 
operate in -40°C to +75°C temperature range.

**Q: Can I turn on MACSec on the switch?**  

A: You need to purchase a **OS-SW-MACSEC** license and install into the switch before you can turn on the MACSec feature.

**Q: What is the difference between OVCX-64-BAS-3Y and OVCX-64-BIZ-3Y?**

A: OVCX-63-BIZ-3Y includes all the features of OVCX-63-BAS-3Y plus Partner Support Plus for the duration of the subscription.  
BIZ and PPXX licenses are mutually exclusive.
