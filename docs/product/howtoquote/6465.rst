OmniSwitch 6465 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6465 series is designed for industrial, transportation, and harsh environment deployments. 
These models are fully hardened, fanless, and support extended temperature ranges, PoE, and flexible mounting.

OS6465-P6
   Hardened fanless Gigabit switch with:
   - 4 × 10/100/1000 RJ-45 PoE+ ports (2 × 60W high PoE)
   - 2 × 100/1000 Base-X SFP ports
   - RS-232 console, alarm relay (1 in / 1 out), USB port  
   Includes user manual, access card, and DIN rail mounting hardware.  
   **Note:** Power supply must be ordered separately.

OS6465H-P12
   Industrial PoE switch with:
   - 8 × 10/100/1000 Base-T RJ-45 (802.3bt Type 3 PoE)
   - 4 × 100/1000 Base-X SFP ports
   - RS-232 console, alarm relay, USB port  
   Supports up to 240W PoE budget (FPoE/PPoE). Power supply ordered separately.

OS6465-P28
   Hardened 1RU fanless switch with:
   - 22 × 10/100/1000 Base-T PoE+ ports (8 × 60W high PoE)
   - 2 × 100/1000 Base-X SFP and 4 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplinks
   - RS-232 console, USB, alarm relay  
   Ships with 19" rack mount kit. **Power supply ordered separately.**

OS6465-P28D
   Same as OS6465-P28, but includes:
   - 24/48V DC power supply and power tray  
   Ships with rack mount kit, manuals, and accessories.

OS6465T-12
   Compact Gigabit chassis (1RU x 1/2 width) with:
   - 8 × RJ-45 Base-T
   - 2 × Gigabit combo ports
   - 2 × SFP ports  
   Includes internal AC power supply, country-specific power cord, SW access card, and RJ45-to-DB9 adapter.  
   **Mounting kits ordered separately.**

OS6465T-P12
   Same form factor as OS6465T-12, with:
   - 8 × 10/100/1000 Base-T PoE+ ports  
   Includes internal AC PSU and accessories. Mounting hardware ordered separately.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What is the difference between T and non-T models?**  
A: The 6465T models are designed for indoor/campus environments with integrated AC power and optional fan operation 
(up to 60°C with fan). The standard OS6465 models are fully hardened, fanless, and support -40°C to +75°C for industrial use.

**Q: Can I enable MACSec on the OmniSwitch 6465?**  
A: Yes, MACSec requires the **OS-SW-MACSEC** license, which must be purchased and installed before activation.

**Q: What is the difference between OVCX-64-BAS and OVCX-64-BIZ?**  
A: OVCX-64-BAS includes cloud management and base support. OVCX-64-BIZ adds Partner Support Plus benefits.
   BIZ and PPxx licenses cannot be combined.
