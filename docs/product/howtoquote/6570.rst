OmniSwitch 6570M Datasheet
===========================

.. contents::
   :local:

Overview
--------

The Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise OmniSwitch 6570M Stackable Gigabit and Multi-Gigabit Ethernet
LAN switch family provides high performance and scalability with a broad range of interface
types and PoE capabilities, making it ideal for enterprise edge, aggregation, and small data
center deployments.

The switches offer high-density multi-gigabit access with 1G, 2.5G, 5G, and 10G ports, and
uplinks up to 25G and 100G. They deliver advanced Layer 2 and Layer 3 switching, low latency,
and comprehensive security features.

Key features include:
- High-density Multi-Gigabit access
- Non-blocking switching capacity
- PoE++ (up to 90W) on access ports
- Hot-swappable, modular power supplies and fan trays
- Stackable up to eight units
- Front-to-back and back-to-front airflow options


Models
------

**OS6570M-P24Z8**

- 24× 1G/2.5G/5G/10G (RJ-45) ports with PoE++ support  
- 8× 25G SFP28 uplinks  
- 2× 100G QSFP28 uplink/stacking ports  
- 600W modular AC power supply  
- Supports up to 1,880W PoE budget  

**OS6570M-P48Z16**

- 48× 1G/2.5G/5G (RJ-45) ports with PoE++ support  
- 16× 25G SFP28 uplinks  
- 2× 100G QSFP28 uplink/stacking ports  
- 920W modular AC power supply  
- Supports up to 1,880W PoE budget  

**OS6570M-X48**

- 48× 10G/25G SFP28 ports  
- 6× 100G QSFP28 uplink/stacking ports  
- Modular AC power supply and fan tray  

**OS6570M-C12**

- 12× 10G/25G SFP28 ports  
- 4× 100G QSFP28 uplink/stacking ports  
- Modular AC power supply and fan tray  


Hardware and Power Options
--------------------------

**Power Supply Units**

- **OS6570M-BP-600W-AC** – 600W modular AC power supply  
- **OS6570M-BP-600W-DC** – 600W modular DC power supply  
- **OS6570M-BP-920W-AC** – 920W modular AC power supply  

All power supplies are hot-swappable and support 1+1 redundancy.

**Fan Trays**

- **OS6570M-FAN-F2B** – Front-to-back airflow  
- **OS6570M-FAN-B2F** – Back-to-front airflow  


Performance
-----------

- Switching capacity: up to 1.2 Tbps  
- Stacking capacity: 480 Gbps  
- MAC addresses: 128K  
- VLANs: 4K  
- Jumbo frames: up to 9,216 bytes  
- Latency: sub-2 microseconds  
- Stack size: up to 8 units  


Environmental and Compliance
----------------------------

- Operating temperature: 0°C to 50°C  
- Storage temperature: -40°C to 70°C  
- Operating humidity: 10% to 90% (non-condensing)  
- Certifications: CE, UL, FCC, RoHS, WEEE  


Management and Software
-----------------------

- Managed by Alcatel-Lucent OmniVista Network Management System  
- Supports OmniVista Cirrus Cloud-based Management  
- Supports CLI, WebView, SNMPv1/v2/v3, and REST APIs  
- Zero-touch provisioning (ZTP) support  


Security
--------

- IEEE 802.1X authentication  
- ACLs and dynamic VLAN assignment  
- Denial of Service (DoS) protection  
- Network Access Control (NAC) integration  


Applications
------------

- High-density multi-gigabit access in campus networks  
- Aggregation for high-performance enterprise backbones  
- Data center Top-of-Rack (ToR) or small spine layer  
- PoE++ power delivery for Wi-Fi 6/6E and IoT devices
