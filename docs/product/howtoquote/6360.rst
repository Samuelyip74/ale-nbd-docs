OmniSwitch 6360 Models
------------------

The OmniSwitch 6360 series includes several models to suit different deployment scenarios. 
Below are the key models and their features:

- OS6360-10: OS6360-10 GigE fixed chassis  8 RJ-45 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 fixed SFP (1G) uplink ports. 1RU size, internal AC power supply. Includes country specific power cord, guides.  Order 19" rack mount kit separately.  
- OS6360-P10: 8 RJ-45 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 fixed SFP (1G) uplink ports. 1RU size, internal AC power supply (120W PoE budget) Includes country specific power cord, guides.  Order 19" rack mount kit separately.  
- OS6360-24: OS6360-24 GigE fixed chassis  24 RJ-45 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP combo (1G), 2 fixed SFP+ (1G/10G) uplink/stacking ports. 1RU size, internal AC power supply. Includes country specific power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount hardware.  
- OS6360-PH24: OS6360-PH24 fixed 1RU chassis 24 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 1G* RJ45/SFP+ combo, 2 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports. Internal AC PSU (380W budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit. *10G license upgradeable.  
- OS6360-P24: OS6360-P24 fixed 1RU chassis 24 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP combo (1G), 2 fixed 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports. Internal AC PSU (180W budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit.  
- OS6360-P24X: OS6360-P24X fixed 1RU chassis 24 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP+ combo (1G/10G), 2 fixed 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports. Internal AC PSU (380 budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit.  
- OS6360-48: OS6360-48 GigE fixed chassis  48 RJ-45 10/100/1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP combo (1G), 2 fixed SFP+ (1G/10G) uplink/stacking ports. 1RU size, internal AC power supply. Includes country specific power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount hardware.  
- OS6360-PH48: OS6360-PH48 fixed 1RU chassis 46 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 PoE 1G/2.5G BaseT, 2 1G* RJ45/SFP+ combo, 2 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports. Internal AC PSU (760W budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit. *10G license upgradeable.  
- OS6360-P48: OS6360-P48 fixed 1RU chassis 48 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP combo (1G), 2 fixed 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports. Internal AC PSU (350W budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit.  
- OS6360-P48X:OS6360-P48X fixed 1RU chassis 48 PoE 1G BaseT, 2 fixed RJ45/SFP+ combo (1G/10G), 2 fixed 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports. Internal AC PSU (760W budget), includes country specific power cord, guides and 19" rack mount kit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------
**Q: How do I mount OS6360-10/P10 on a 19" Rack?**  

A: You need to purchase **OS6360-RM-19-L** in order to mount OS6360-10/P10 on a 19" Rack.

**Q: My customer do not have a 19" Rack, what are my options to mount the switch?**  

A: You can mount the switch on a flat surface wall using the **OS6360-WALL-MNT** mounting kit.

**Q: When do I need to purchase the OS6360-SW-PERF license?**  

A: You need to purchase the OS6360-SW-PERF license when you want to enable the 10G uplink ports on the OS6360-PH24 or OS6360-PH48 models.
