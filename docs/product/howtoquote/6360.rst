Audience & Outcomes
-------------------
**Audience:** Presales engineers, solution consultants, and channel partners.

**Outcomes:** By the end of this guide you will be able to:
- Select the right products and licenses for a customer use case.
- Produce a complete and supportable Bill of Materials (BOM).
- Apply pricing, discounts, and services correctly.
- Pass internal review and hand off cleanly to Sales and Delivery.

Prerequisites
-------------
- Confirmed customer **use case**, **site count**, **users/devices**, **performance targets**, and **growth horizon** (12–36 months).
- Known **regional constraints** (certifications, import, lead times).
- Access to current **price list**, **discount policy**, and **SKU catalog** (see :ref:`pricing-refs`).

Quoting Workflow (At a Glance)
------------------------------
1. **Qualify scope** → capture requirements and constraints.
2. **Size the solution** → map needs to product families and license tiers.
3. **Assemble BOM** → hardware, licenses, software subscriptions, services.
4. **Validate** → compatibility, power, optics, quantities, spares.
5. **Price & Discount** → apply bands, programs, and promotions.
6. **Review & Approve** → technical + commercial checks.
7. **Deliver Quote Pack** → BOM, pricing, assumptions, diagrams, T&Cs.

.. tip::
   Save time with the :ref:`quote-checklist` before requesting approvals.

Sizing & Selection
------------------
Use this section to translate requirements into the right product mix.

**Inputs to collect**
- Sites & topology: campus, branch, DC, trackside, onboard, IoT.
- Scale: users/ports/APs, throughput, latency, redundancy.
- Services: management platform, analytics, security features.
- Environmental: temp range, MTBF, form factor, PoE budget.

**Selection rules of thumb**
- **Edge**: pick switches/APs by port type/PoE class and density.
- **Aggregation/Core**: size for peak *x* 1.5 headroom, HA/stacking.
- **Wireless**: AP model by client mix and RF design; controller/subscription alignment.
- **Licensing**: align feature tier to required capabilities only; avoid over-tiering.
- **Optics & cabling**: match reach/data rate, check DDM and vendor support.

Licensing & Subscriptions
-------------------------
Document what each tier unlocks and how it’s counted.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 25 25

   * - Tier
     - Capabilities
     - Metering
     - Co-Term Policy
   * - Base
     - Monitoring, inventory, basic config
     - Per device
     - Align to support contract date
   * - Advanced
     - Automation, analytics, API, security features
     - Per device or per user (if NAC)
     - Auto-adjust on adds/moves
   * - Premium
     - Full feature set incl. compliance/reporting
     - Per device + data retention
     - True-up quarterly

.. warning::
   Always confirm **subscription alignment**: device count == license count, term matches **deal close + lead time + deployment window**.

Optics, Cables & Power Budget
-----------------------------
- Validate **uplink speeds** and **port types** at both ends.
- Compute **PoE**: sum device draw at *realistic* class, add 20–30% headroom.
- Include **redundant PSUs** and **power cords** per regional standard.

Pricing & Discounting
---------------------
- Start from **book price** (current list). Apply **programmatic discounts** by band and region.
- Account for **promo SKUs** or bundles when applicable.
- Add **services** margins per policy.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 25 25 25

   * - Band
     - Eligibility
     - Typical Range
     - Notes
   * - Standard
     - All partners
     - 0–15%
     - Small deals or renewals
   * - Growth
     - Registered, strategic accounts
     - 15–30%
     - Competitive motions
   * - Strategic
     - Net-new logos / lighthouse
     - 30–40%+
     - Requires VP approval

.. important::
   Stack **only** compatible programs. Document assumptions in the quote cover page.

Commercial Terms & T&Cs
-----------------------
Include or attach:
- **Validity** (e.g., 30 days), **payment terms**, **Incoterms**, **delivery lead time**.
- **Warranty** and **support SLAs** by product family.
- **EOL/EOS** statements if refresh cycles are relevant.
- **Exclusions & assumptions**: cabling, civil works, RF survey, etc.

Technical & Commercial Validation
---------------------------------
- Compatibility: software versions, license tiers, optics.
- Capacity: throughput, PoE, backplane, stacking limits.
- Redundancy: PSU, fan, uplinks, HA features.
- Services: scope, travel, milestones, acceptance criteria.

Quote Checklist (Copy/Paste)
----------------------------
- [ ] Scope confirmed (sites, users, growth).
- [ ] Correct models and regulatory variants.
- [ ] Optics/cables complete (with spares).
- [ ] PoE and power budgets validated.
- [ ] Licenses/subscriptions aligned and co-termed.
- [ ] Services scoped and scheduled.
- [ ] Pricing bands and promos applied; margin OK.
- [ ] T&Cs attached (validity, Incoterms, SLA).

Internal Reviews & Approvals
----------------------------
- **Technical Review:** solution fit, risk, supportability.
- **Commercial Review:** pricing, margin, terms, credit.
- **Special Approvals:** strategic band, non-standard T&Cs, custom SKUs.

Deliverables & Handover
-----------------------
Provide a **quote pack** with:
- **Executive summary** (1 page): scope, value, outcomes.
- **BOM & pricing** (CSV/PDF).
- **Diagrams** (topology, rack elevations if needed).
- **Assumptions & exclusions**.
- **Implementation plan & services SOW**.
- **SKU datasheets** (links to :ref:`datasheets-index`).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------
**Q: How do I co-term licenses when adding devices mid-term?**  
A: Prorate to the master end date; use add-on SKUs where available.

**Q: Do I need optics spares?**  
A: Yes—carry ~10% spares per optic type per site.

**Q: Who approves Strategic band discounts?**  
A: Follow the :ref:`approval-matrix` for thresholds and sign-offs.
