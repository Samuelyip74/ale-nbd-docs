OmniSwitch 6360 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6360 series includes multiple models designed for small to mid-sized enterprise deployments, 
offering a balance of Gigabit access, PoE options, and flexible uplink capabilities.

OS6360-10
   Fixed Gigabit model with 8 × 10/100/1000 Base-T ports, 2 × 10/100/1000 Base-T ports, 
   and 2 × 1G SFP uplink ports.  
   1RU size with internal AC power supply. Includes country-specific power cord and guides.  
   **Note:** 19" rack mount kit must be ordered separately.

OS6360-P10
   PoE version of OS6360-10 with 8 × 10/100/1000 Base-T, 2 × 10/100/1000 Base-T, and 2 × 1G SFP uplink ports.  
   Internal AC power supply with **120W PoE budget**.  
   **Note:** 19" rack mount kit must be ordered separately.

OS6360-24
   24 × 10/100/1000 Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP combo (1G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Includes internal AC PSU, power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount hardware.

OS6360-PH24
   24 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × 1G RJ-45/SFP+ combo ports*, and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **380W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.  
   *10G support upgradeable via license.

OS6360-P24
   24 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP combo (1G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **180W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.

OS6360-P24X
   24 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP+ combo (1G/10G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **380W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.

OS6360-48
   48 × 10/100/1000 Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP combo (1G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Includes internal AC PSU, power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount hardware.

OS6360-PH48
   46 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × PoE 1G/2.5G Base-T ports, 2 × 1G RJ-45/SFP+ combo*, and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **760W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.  
   *10G support upgradeable via license.

OS6360-P48
   48 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP combo (1G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/stacking ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **350W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.

OS6360-P48X
   48 × PoE 1G Base-T ports, 2 × RJ-45/SFP+ combo (1G/10G), and 2 × 1G/10G SFP+ uplink/VFL ports.  
   Internal AC PSU with **760W PoE budget**. Includes power cord, guides, and 19" rack mount kit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: How do I mount OS6360-10/P10 on a 19" rack?**  

A: Purchase the **OS6360-RM-19-L** rack mount kit.

**Q: My customer does not have a 19" rack. What are the mounting alternatives?**  

A: Use the **OS6360-WALL-MNT** kit to mount the switch on a flat surface or wall.

**Q: When is the OS6360-SW-PERF license required?**  

A: The OS6360-SW-PERF license is required to enable 10G uplink ports on OS6360-PH24 or OS6360-PH48 models.

**Q: What is the difference between OVCX-63-BAS-3Y and OVCX-63-BIZ-3Y?**  

A: OVCX-63-BIZ-3Y includes all features of BAS plus Partner Support Plus.  
   BAS and BIZ licenses are mutually exclusive.
