OmniSwitch 9900 Chassis Overview
--------------------------------

The OmniSwitch 9900 is a high-capacity, modular core switching platform designed for enterprise
campus backbones, large-scale aggregation, and data center fabric environments. It supports advanced
AOS features such as SPB Fabric, EVPN/VXLAN, MPLS, VRF, SDN, MACsec, and high PoE delivery.

**Chassis Models**

OS9907
   7-slot (11RU) modular chassis supporting up to 5 line cards, dual CMM, and redundant AC/DC power.
   Ideal for campus core or high-powered PoE aggregation.

OS9912
   12-slot (16RU) modular chassis with up to 10 line cards. Higher capacity fabric for large campus/distribution
   networks or fabric core deployments.

**Control and Management Modules (CMM)**

OS9900-CMM
   Control and fabric module providing central switching, routing, and fabric services.
   Supports hitless failover with 1+1 redundancy.

**Line Card Options**

OS9900-X20
   20-port SFP+ (10G) line card for high-density aggregation or L3 services.

OS9900-X40
   40-port SFP+ (10G) line card for maximum fiber density.

OS9900-Q8
   8-port QSFP (40G) card for spine/leaf uplinks or inter-chassis stacking.

OS9900-Z24
   24-port PoE+ / PoE++ (802.3at/bt) copper line card with 10G/25G capability (campus core edge).

**Power Supply Options**

OS9900-PS-AC
   AC power supply module for campus installations.

OS9900-PS-DC
   DC power input module for telco and industrial deployments.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What fabric and routing capabilities does OS9900 support?**  
A: OS9900 supports SPB-M, EVPN/VXLAN, MPLS, VRF, and advanced routing (BGP, OSPF, IS-IS) via Metro/Fabric
or Advanced Routing (AR) license.

**Q: Does OS9900 support PoE for access aggregation?**  
A: Yes. OS9900-Z24 line cards provide PoE+/PoE++ (up to 90W) for campus edge APs, cameras, and IoT devices.

**Q: What redundancy options are available?**  
A: The system supports dual CMM, hot-swappable PSU, hitless failover, and Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation (MC-LAG)
for sub-50ms resilience.

**Q: Is MACsec supported?**  
A: Yes. MACsec (802.1AE) is available on fiber and copper via license, depending on line card capabilities.

**Q: Can OS9900 be cloud-managed via OmniVista Cirrus?**  
A: Yes. OVCX-65 licenses allow zero-touch provisioning, telemetry, and remote monitoring via OmniVista Cirrus 10.

**Q: Are DC power options available?**  
A: Yes. Use OS9900-PS-DC modules for telco or industrial sites requiring battery-backed DC input.
