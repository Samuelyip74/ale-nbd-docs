OmniSwitch 6900 Models
----------------------

The OmniSwitch 6900 family is a high-performance, low-latency switching platform designed for enterprise core,
data center leaf-spine fabrics, and high-density campus backbones. It supports advanced Ethernet services,
Layer 2/3 fabric technologies (SPB, EVPN/VXLAN), MACsec, and both AC/DC power.

**Model Series Overview**

- **X & T Series – Campus / Core Edge**
  Fixed 10G/25G switching for aggregation and high-density campus core.
  
- **V & C Series – Fabric Spine / Data Center**
  High-performance models with 40G/100G uplinks for spine/core roles.

**X & T Series (Campus Core Aggregation)**

OS6900-X24
   24 × 10G SFP+ ports, 2 × QSFP+ uplinks (40G), ultra-low latency.  
   Ideal for campus core or collapsed distribution layer.

OS6900-X48
   48 × 10G SFP+ ports, 4 × 40G QSFP uplinks.  
   Supports SPB, VRF, MACsec, and VXLAN with license.

OS6900-T24
   24 × 10G Base-T (RJ-45) + 2 × QSFP+ uplinks (40G).  
   Suitable for mixed fiber/copper aggregation.

OS6900-T48
   48 × 10G Base-T + 4 × 40G QSFP uplinks.  
   Ideal for campus backbone modernization.

**V & C Series (Fabric Spine / Data Center)**

OS6900-V48
   48 × 10G SFP+ ports + 6 × 40G/100G QSFP uplinks.  
   Supports EVPN/VXLAN, SPB Fabric, MPLS, and Data Center bridging.

OS6900-C32
   32 × 100G QSFP28 ports, spine-ready platform.  
   Optimized for large-scale fabric, MACsec, and distributed core.

OS6900-V72
   72 × 10G SFP+ ports + 6 × 100G QSFP uplinks.  
   Combines access density with high-speed fabric aggregation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
--------------------------------

**Q: What advanced fabric features are supported on the OmniSwitch 6900?**  
A: OS6900 supports SPB Fabric, EVPN/VXLAN, MPLS, VRF, and Layer 2/3 virtualization.  
Fabric features require the Metro/Fabric or Advanced Routing (AR) license.

**Q: Does OS6900 support MACsec encryption?**  
A: Yes. MACsec (802.1AE) is supported on both fiber (SFP+/QSFP+) and copper ports (on T models) with the proper license.

**Q: What are the power options for OS6900?**  
A: Most OS6900 models support hot-swappable AC or DC power supplies.  
Select DC PSU for data center or telco deployments.

**Q: Can OS6900 be managed via OmniVista Cirrus Cloud?**  
A: Yes. Use **OVCX-69-BAS** or **OVCX-69-BIZ** for cloud-based provisioning, telemetry, and lifecycle management.

**Q: How is resiliency implemented on OS6900?**  
A: Supports Virtual Chassis, Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation (MC-LAG), fast failover, and 40G/100G redundant fabric links.

**Q: Is VXLAN supported on OS6900?**  
A: Yes. VXLAN bridging and EVPN control-plane are available with the proper software license.
